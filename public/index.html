    How it works

    1. Real scan and task test

      The open-source axe-core engine checks WCAG 2.2 A/AA rules. Then OpenRamp tests your key customer task by pressing Tab like a keyboard user and listening like a screen reader, which catches problems rule scanners miss, such as invisible keyboard focus.

    2. AI proposes fixes

      Claude reads the findings, checks colour contrast with exact maths, looks at your images to write descriptions, and explains each fix in plain language.

    3. You approve

      Nothing is applied without your approval, and your live website is never changed.

    4. Proof

      Approved fixes are applied to a copy and re-scanned, so you see what actually improved.

    Why it matters: 95.9% of the top one million home pages have detectable accessibility failures (WebAIM Million, 2026). That means customers who use screen readers, have low vision or can't use a mouse often can't order from you.
